Parmatma Ek — Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Our commitment

Parmatma Ek is committed to maintaining a safe community and does not permit child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, sextortion, trafficking, or other conduct that endangers children.

Prohibited content and behaviour

Users must not create, upload, share, request, promote, or distribute content or behaviour that sexually exploits or abuses children. Such content or behaviour is prohibited on Parmatma Ek.

Reporting concerns

Users can report safety concerns through the reporting/feedback mechanism available inside the Parmatma Ek app. Reports concerning child safety are treated seriously and may result in removal of content, account restrictions, or other appropriate action.

Action on CSAM or CSAE

When Parmatma Ek obtains actual knowledge of prohibited child sexual abuse material or child exploitation, appropriate action will be taken, which may include removing the content, restricting or terminating accounts, preserving relevant information as required by law, and reporting to appropriate authorities where legally required.

Child safety contact

For child-safety concerns, contact the designated support email shown in the Parmatma Ek app or the developer contact information associated with the app's Google Play listing.

Legal compliance

Parmatma Ek will cooperate with applicable child-safety laws and lawful requests from relevant authorities.

Scope

These standards apply to users and content on Parmatma Ek and are intended to clearly prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation.
